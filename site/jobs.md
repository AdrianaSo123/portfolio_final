You bring the creative domain expertise that turns a technically correct system into one that creative professionals will actually use . 

What You'll Do 

Design creative workflows. Map end-to-end content production pipelines — from creative brief through asset delivery. Identify where AI can accelerate, automate, or enhance each step. 

Build templates and frameworks. Create reusable creative templates (InDesign, After Effects, Photoshop, Figma), asset structures, and production frameworks that scale across customer engagements.

Deep creative production experience. 3-7+ years in creative production, post-production, motion graphics, content operations, or creative technology. You've worked in agencies, studios, or in-house creative teams producing real content at scale.

Workflow design thinking. You've designed or optimized content production pipelines. You understand DAM systems, asset management, review/approval workflows, versioning, localization, and multi-channel delivery.
